Troubleshooting

If Forward Email is not working, these steps can help. Your mail is stored on the server and is not affected by anything on this page.

Open Forward Email

Start the app normally again.

Copy diagnostics

Copies a short report and the app's recent log so you can paste it into an email to support. The log has passwords and keys removed.

Sign out and reset

Signs out of every account and removes all data this app keeps on this computer: cached mail, settings, unsent drafts that were never saved to the server, and any PGP private keys imported into the app. Export those keys first if you need them. You can sign in again afterwards.

This cannot be undone.
